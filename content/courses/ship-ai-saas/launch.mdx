## Launching and getting your first customers

Shipping is the start, not the finish. Here is how to get the first paying users.

### Pick an ultra-narrow niche

"Summarize anything" is a feature. "Summarize commercial leases for solo
realtors" is a business. Narrow audiences are reachable, referrable, and willing
to pay more.

### Launch checklist

- A landing page that speaks to one painful problem
- A free tier that delivers a real taste of value
- A Product Hunt / community launch with a short demo video
- Founder-led posting where your niche already gathers

### After launch

Talk to every early user. The first ten conversations tell you what to build,
what to charge, and which words convert. Iterate on those, not on vanity metrics.
