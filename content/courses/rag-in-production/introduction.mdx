## Why naive RAG fails

The demo always works. Then real users arrive and the assistant confidently
makes things up. This free intro explains why, and what this course fixes.

### The three failure modes

1. **Bad retrieval** — the right context never makes it into the prompt.
2. **No grounding** — the model answers from training data instead of your docs.
3. **No measurement** — you can't tell if a change made things better or worse.

### What you'll learn

A retrieval pipeline that actually surfaces the right chunks, prompting that
keeps answers grounded, and an evaluation harness so you ship improvements with
confidence instead of vibes.
