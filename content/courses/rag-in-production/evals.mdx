## Evaluating answer quality

If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. Evals turn RAG from guesswork into
engineering.

### Build a golden set

Collect 20–50 real questions with known-good answers. This is your regression
suite — small enough to maintain, large enough to catch breakage.

### Score what matters

- **Faithfulness** — is the answer supported by the retrieved context?
- **Relevance** — does it actually answer the question?
- **Retrieval hit rate** — was the needed chunk retrieved at all?

### Close the loop

Run the evals on every change to prompts, chunking, or models. Ship only when the
numbers hold or improve. This discipline is the difference between a demo and a
product.
